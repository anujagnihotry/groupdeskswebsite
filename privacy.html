Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

What we collect

When you fill in the form on this website we collect your name, email address, WhatsApp number, business name and team size. When you message us on WhatsApp or email us, we receive the details you send.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and set up your account.
  • To send you a confirmation email and contact you on WhatsApp or email about GroupDesks.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use Resend to send emails, Railway to host this website, and Cloudflare to work out your country from your IP address so we can show prices in the right currency. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Local storage

If you choose a currency on the pricing section, your browser remembers it so we can show it next time. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@groupdesks.com.
