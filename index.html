    FAQ

    Questions

    Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

    No. You link your existing business WhatsApp number, and your current chats and groups show up in GroupDesks.

    Can several people use the same number at the same time?

    Yes. Each teammate signs in with their own account, from any location, on web or mobile, and works the same chats.

    Will customers see who on my team replied?

    Customers see your business number as usual. Inside GroupDesks your team sees who replied and can leave internal notes the customer never sees.

    Does it work with WhatsApp groups?

    Yes. Groups can be assigned to desks, filtered for messages that need a reply, and managed together.
